About the lab

Our Vision

At SAIL Lab, we are on a journey to broaden the participation of underrepresented student populations in education through innovative applications of technology and artificial intelligence. We are sailing toward the creation of inclusive, sustainable, and equitable learning environments, with the ultimate goal of reducing academic disparities.

Research framework

What guides our work

Context

Research Context

  • AI-enhanced Learning Environments. Exploring the integration of AI tools to transform classrooms.
  • Technology Integration. Fostering innovative uses of technology to support learning across subjects.
  • Computer Science Education. Promoting equitable access and engagement in CS learning for all students.

Approach

Research Approach

  • Personalized Learning. Leveraging AI and technology to tailor learning experiences to individual student needs.
  • Student-Centered Learning. Prioritizing student agency, voice, and participation in shaping their own learning pathways.
  • Culturally-Relevant Pedagogy. [Add a one-line description for this approach.]

Results

Research Goals

  • Broadening Participation. Increasing involvement of underrepresented and struggling students in education.
  • Reducing Learning Disparities. Addressing gaps in educational achievement through innovative approaches.
  • Inclusive & Sustainable Education. Ensuring educational innovations are accessible, equitable, and sustainable for diverse learners.

Director · Principal Investigator

Dr. Hyejeong Lee

Assistant Professor of Learning, Design, and Technology · UTSA

Dr. Lee earned her Ph.D. in Instructional Systems Technology from Indiana University Bloomington. She holds a bachelor's degree in Elementary Education and Computer Science Education and a master's degree in Educational Technology. Through long-term projects that leverage AI technologies, she aims to support struggling and underrepresented students and to promote diversity and inclusion in education.

PhD Candidate · UTSA

Donna Dancer

Third-year PhD candidate in Interdisciplinary Learning and Teaching, focused on Learning, Design, and Technology. Her expertise lies in child, family, and human development as a lifelong educational experience.

Donna earned her M.A. in Education at UTSA in the Learning, Design, and Technology cognate. As a graduate assistant she studied high school teachers' perceptions of augmented reality as an interdisciplinary practice in STEAM. She continues to research artificial intelligence, data science, and functional digital literacies.

PhD Candidate · UTSA

Wei Yan

PhD candidate in Interdisciplinary Learning and Teaching, concentrating in Learning, Design, and Technology. She studies technology in K-12 special education, learning analytics, and serious games for students with learning disabilities.

She aims to bridge research and practice by developing practical, technology-driven solutions that can be readily implemented in K-12 inclusive classrooms.

PhD Student · UTSA

Cyndi R. Piña, M.Ed., MFA

Cyndi Piña is a PhD student in Interdisciplinary Learning and Teaching at the University of Texas at San Antonio. She brings over 20 years of experience in K–12 education as a classroom teacher, instructional leader, and educational consultant.

As founder of As You Read and Write, LLC, she has partnered with schools and districts to strengthen literacy instruction, support teacher professional learning, and foster meaningful student engagement.

Her research interests center on how instructional environments, teacher decision-making, and opportunities for student agency shape engagement, literacy development, and equitable learning experiences. Through her work with SAIL Lab, she is exploring how artificial intelligence and emerging educational technologies intersect with these interests, particularly in relation to teacher professional learning and inclusive instructional practices.

Drawing on her experience bridging educational theory and classroom practice, Cyndi seeks to contribute to research that promotes thoughtful, inclusive, and equitable approaches to teaching and learning.

SAIL: Sustainable AI and Inclusive Learning

The SAIL Project is dedicated to broadening the participation of underrepresented student populations in education through the innovative use of technology and artificial intelligence. It aims to create inclusive, sustainable, and equitable learning environments, ultimately working to reduce academic disparities.

By integrating AI and cutting-edge technologies into educational practices, the project explores innovative strategies for personalized learning, teacher training, and classroom transformation, driving meaningful change in education systems.

Other research projects

Projects Dr. Lee has contributed to, with their funders.

Funded by NSF

IntegrateAI

Inquiry-driven projects where middle school students investigate scientific questions using natural language data, building knowledge and skills through Natural Language Processing (NLP) experiences. The project also helps teachers develop AI competencies so they feel prepared to bring inquiry-based NLP into their classrooms.

Funded by NSF

PrimaryAI

Focuses on AI in elementary education, creating engaging learning experiences that integrate AI and life science through immersive problem-based learning.

Funded by Google

CS for ED: National CS Education Case Studies

Creating a public repository that serves as ongoing inspiration for teacher education programs as they work to include computer science.

Funded by the Korean Ministry of Education

Teacher Education in Generative AI

A professional development program that builds teachers' ability to implement AI-integrated curricula, while examining teachers' professional development needs and considerations for AI-integrated classes.

Funded by Lilly Endowment

Charting the Future

Creating classrooms that produce equitable outcomes and optimizing support for critical student populations on campus, including redesigning STEM and gateway courses for greater student success, equity, and belonging.

Funded by the U.S. Department of Education

PBL Tech

Developing problem-based learning (PBL) video cases that help educators implement the PBL approach and build effective problem-solving skills.

Research project

CS Experiences in High School

Examining high school students' positive and negative experiences in computer science to broaden CS participation for underrepresented students.

Research project · CEWIT

Digital Survival Skills for Women

Helping female students improve their digital literacy skills. As a Digital Expert researcher at CEWIT, the Center of Excellence for Women & Technology, Dr. Lee encouraged women to pursue technology to increase diversity in computer science.

Research project

Online Education and Assessment

Examining online and distance education with a focus on assessment, including research on technology-enhanced assessments such as ChatGPT.
